## 🌟 About

**AlHayat AI Restaurant** is a modern AI-powered restaurant web application designed to make food ordering smarter, easier, and more convenient.

It brings restaurant services together through an interactive platform where customers can explore food, place orders, track deliveries, reserve tables, and get intelligent food recommendations.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🍕 **Interactive Food Menu** – Explore food items, categories, prices and descriptions.
- 🛒 **Smart Cart & Checkout** – Add items, manage quantities and place food orders.
- 🤖 **AI Food Assistant** – Get intelligent food recommendations and suggestions.
- 📦 **Order Tracking** – Track orders and check their current status.
- 📅 **Table Reservation** – Book tables with customer and booking details.
- 👨‍🍳 **Career Portal** – Apply for restaurant jobs and upload resumes.
- 👤 **User Authentication** – Customer registration and login.
- 🛠️ **Admin Management** – Manage restaurant operations and customer orders.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Explore:** Customers visit the website and browse the food menu.
2. **Choose:** Select favourite dishes and add them to the shopping cart.
3. **Order:** Enter delivery details and proceed to checkout.
4. **Payment:** Select an available payment method.
5. **Track:** View order details and follow order progress.
6. **Discover:** Use the AI Food Assistant for personalized food suggestions.

## 💻 Technologies Used

**Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap

**Backend:** Node.js, Express.js

**Database:** MongoDB, Mongoose

**Additional Technologies:** Socket.IO, REST APIs, Vercel

## 🎯 Project Vision

To transform traditional restaurant services into a smarter digital experience by combining modern web technologies, AI-powered assistance, and convenient online food ordering.
